Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

ATS Resume Maker ("we", "us") is operated by Mayuri Kapil Dhage, India. This policy explains how we handle your information.

What we collect

  • Resume content you upload or paste (such as name, contact details, work history and education).
  • Payment details are handled by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive only a payment ID and status.

How we use it

  • To convert your resume into an ATS-friendly format and generate your PDF.
  • To process your payment and respond to support requests.

Third-party services

Resume text is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to format it, and payments are processed by Razorpay. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Storage

We do not intentionally store your resume on our servers. Your browser holds the content while you edit it. Hosting and payment providers may keep technical logs.

Your choices

You can stop using the service at any time. For any privacy question or request, email kapildhage30@gmail.com.
